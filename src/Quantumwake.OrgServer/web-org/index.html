Quantum Wake org server

This is an org space for Quantum Wake, the Star Citizen log-reading dashboard. Members link their own app here and choose, class by class, what to share with their org. Nothing arrives that somebody did not click to send, and everything a member shared can be deleted by that member.

To join: install Quantum Wake, put this server's address in its Settings page, link the app, and paste the invite code your org gave you.
