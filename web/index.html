Every movement the logs record, newest first. There is no wallet event in Game.log, so this is a record of what went out and came in — not a balance. Item purchases are confirmed by the server; cargo trades are amounts requested at the kiosk, marked with ~.

Equipment by slot

What was last equipped in each slot. Numbered siblings are merged — nine magazine ports read as one row with counts — and slots that are not really equipment are left out: the character model, the mobiGlas and radar fixtures, and the hands, which hold things transiently rather than equip them.
