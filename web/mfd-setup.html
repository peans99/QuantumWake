How the shipped profile is laid out

Top buttons 1–5 select Nav, Task, Act, Cargo and Contract; left button 16 selects Status. Nav shows location, destination and ship. Task shows the next planned stop and its next unfinished instruction. Act lists the outstanding work at that stop and lets you tick one off — press DONE once to arm and again to confirm. Cargo shows what your plan says to load against what the commodity counters recorded this session, and never claims to know what is in the hold. Contract shows the contract accepted in this session and its objective count. Status keeps the latest screenshot reading with its capture time.

Bottom 15 / 11: previous / next page; 14 / 12: up and down, which move the cursor on Act and scroll everywhere else; 13: Home. Right 6 / 7: text size; 8: DONE. Left 20 / 19: screen brightness.

The four rocker switches report as buttons 21–28, and which rocker is which number is not something a datasheet answers — so they ship unassigned. Press one, watch the tester name its number, and bind it above.

USB detection uses Windows joystick slots 1–16 and the default F16 MFD names. If a device is missing, check the Thrustmaster driver and close any T.A.R.G.E.T. profile that replaces the devices with a virtual controller. Duplicate MFD numbers must be changed in the Thrustmaster control panel.

MFD mode is optional. Your regular overlay has its own settings.
